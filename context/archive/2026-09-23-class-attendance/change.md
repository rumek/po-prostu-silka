## Notes

Roadmap item S-27 (M-8 `persona-class-day`), scope anchors AT-01–AT-05 in the M-8 charter of
`context/foundation/roadmap.md`.

User's decisions (2026-09-23, Polish, recorded in English):

- Attendance is marked by the trainer for the classes they instruct and by an admin for any class.
- A karnet entry is spent when the member attends, not when they are booked.
- The member sees their own attendance history, and it must be presented well from a UI/UX point of
  view, not as a wall of text.
- Out of scope: walk-ins without a booking, member self check-in, attendance statistics.
